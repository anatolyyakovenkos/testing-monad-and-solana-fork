about

The people of Iran deserve to be seen. For too long, headlines have reduced an entire nation to politics and conflict. But Iran is its people — millions of real humans living real lives.

The Hand is our answer. A hand drawn by real people, in cities across the world. It's intentionally simple. It doesn't need to be art. It just needs to carry the message: the world sees you.

Every time someone buys or sells $HAND on pump.fun, creator fees flow into our vault. We use those fees to pay locals in countries around the world to draw our hand symbol in their city. No buybacks. No treasury hoarding. Every fee goes to spreading the hand.

The Hand isn't about politics. It's about people. It's about showing that a simple drawing can carry a powerful message. That solidarity doesn't need to be complicated.

Every rough sketch, every chalk drawing on a wall — these aren't just doodles. They're acts of visibility. Proof that people around the world are seen, heard, and supported. The hand keeps spreading, one country at a time, funded entirely by creator fees.

creator fees

Every swap on pump.fun generates creator fees. We collect those fees and use them to fund hand drawings in new countries. Here's how they're allocated:

allocation share
Drawing the hand in new countries 50%
Buybacks 25%

50% goes directly to paying people around the world to draw the hand in their city. We start with Iran and expand to every country we can reach.

25% goes to buybacks to support the token and reward holders who believe in the mission.

25% goes to the dev team to coordinate artists and keep the project running.
