about

The people of Iran deserve to be seen. For too long, headlines have reduced an entire nation to politics and conflict. But Iran is its people — millions of real humans living real lives.

The Hand is our answer. A stick man, drawn by hand, in cities across Iran. It's intentionally simple. It doesn't need to be art. It just needs to carry the message: the world sees you.

We paid real people in cities across Iran to draw the stick man in their neighborhoods. Not because it's art, but because it's a message. Each drawing is unique. Each carries the courage of the person who made it.

The Hand isn't about politics. It's about people. It's about showing that a simple drawing can carry a powerful message. That solidarity doesn't need to be complicated. That the world sees Iran.

Every wobbly line, every rough sketch, every chalk drawing on a wall — these aren't just doodles. They're acts of visibility. They're proof that the people of Iran are seen, heard, and supported by people around the world.
